Disks, and where things are kept

Everything on this machine lives under a mount, and a path always starts with one. df lists them.

/rom is built in and read-only. The kernel, the apps, this manual and the examples are here, and no amount of deleting will hurt it.

/disk is this machine's own hard drive, if it has one. Your files, your saved games and the desktop's own notes go here.

/fd0 and /fd1 are floppies in disk drives. /cd0 and /cd1 are CDs. A CD is read-only; a floppy is not.

Put a Disk Drive block next to the computer, or on the same bus cable, and whatever you put in it turns up as a mount within a second or two. Take it out and it goes away again -- so do not leave a program's only copy on a floppy you are about to carry off.

A disk that was written by another computer is refused until you format it -- the Files app has the button, and formatting erases it, which is the whole warning. df is where you look when you want to know how full something is.

Files also burns a blank CD: put one in a drive, choose a mount, and it copies the lot onto the CD for good. That is how you hand a program to somebody else.
